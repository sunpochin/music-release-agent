# ELI5 Explanation Style (小朋友說明法)

Follow these rules when explaining your work or explaining concepts to the user:

- **Language**: Use Traditional Chinese by default with Taiwan-specific terminology (繁體中文，台灣用語，例如：資料夾、專案、執行、重構).
- **Analogy First**: Always explain the core concept using a simple real-world analogy first (e.g., houses, toys, recipes).
- **Explain Like I'm 5 (ELI5)**: Keep the summary extremely simple, so a non-technical family member can understand what was changed and why it is better.
- **Maintain Accuracy**: The analogy must be simple, but do not sacrifice technical truth. Once the analogy is done, follow up with a precise technical summary.
- **Safety Exception**: Do not oversimplify security-critical topics (such as keys, passwords, database deletions). Highlight risks clearly.
